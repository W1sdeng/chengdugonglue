Essay 1

My Free Time

90 words

对应 Unit 1 Time to Relax（休闲放松）  语法重点：动词不定式 like / want to do

写作提示

  • 想一想你平时放学后和周末都做什么：运动、看电视、读书、画画……
  • 用 I like to ... 和 I want to ... 说出自己喜欢和想做的事。
  • 写一两句为什么喜欢，或这件事对你的好处。
  • 结尾用一句话总结：自由时间很重要。

Everyone needs free time. After school, I like to play basketball with my friends. We usually play on the playground for an hour. I want to be a good player, so I practice every day. Sometimes I like to watch TV, but my mother doesn't want me to watch too much. She says I need to read books. I want to learn to play the guitar this summer. It is difficult but fun. Free time makes me happy. I think it is important to have some free time every day.

（可贴照片或自己画图）

亮点句型 Highlighted Sentences

  • I like to play basketball —— like to do sth. 表达“喜欢做某事”
  • I want to be a good player / I want to learn to play the guitar —— want to do sth. 表达“想要做某事”
  • my mother doesn't want me to watch too much —— want sb. to do sth. “想让某人做某事”
  • It is important to have some free time —— It is + 形容词 + to do sth. 句型
Essay 2

How to Stay Healthy

103 words

对应 Unit 2 Stay Healthy（保持健康）  语法重点：should 提建议 · 反身代词

写作提示

  • 先总说健康很重要，再分点写建议：饮食、运动、睡眠、少玩手机。
  • 用 should / shouldn't 提出建议。
  • 加进反身代词（by myself, ourselves, enjoy themselves）让句子更地道。
  • 结尾鼓励大家养成好习惯。

We all want to stay healthy. Here are my ideas. First, you should eat healthy food. You should eat more fruit and vegetables, and you should drink milk every day. My mother always tells me to drink milk. Second, you should exercise. I usually run in the morning by myself. Sometimes I hurt myself, so I should be careful. Third, you should go to bed early. Don't stay up too late. Also, you shouldn't spend too much time on your phone. We should take care of ourselves. If you do these things, you will be healthy and strong. I hope everyone can enjoy themselves.

Picture Place
（可贴照片或自己画图）

亮点句型 Highlighted Sentences

  • you should eat healthy food / you should exercise —— should + 动词原形 提出建议
  • I run in the morning by myself —— 反身代词 myself（by oneself 独自）
  • I hurt myself —— 反身代词 myself 作宾语
  • take care of ourselves / everyone can enjoy themselves —— 反身代词 ourselves / themselves
Essay 3

Growing Up

111 words

对应 Unit 3 Growing Up（成长）  语法重点：代词与不定代词

写作提示

  • 写成长中的变化：长高了、不再怕黑、能做更多事、学会帮助家人。
  • 用 used to 对比“过去”和“现在”。
  • 提到父母和老师的帮助，表达感谢。
  • 结尾表达自己愿意继续成长的心愿。

Growing up is not easy, but I have learned a lot. When I was a child, I was afraid of the dark. I couldn't sleep alone. Now I can do many things by myself. I used to be short and thin. Now I am taller than before. My clothes don't fit me any more. I know more about the world, but I also have more problems. Sometimes I don't know what to do. But my parents and teachers always help me. They tell me that everyone has something to learn. I think I am growing up. I want to do something useful for my family. Growing up is a beautiful thing.

Picture Place
（可贴照片或自己画图）

亮点句型 Highlighted Sentences

  • I used to be short and thin —— used to do sth. 表示“过去常常……”
  • I am taller than before —— 形容词比较级（taller than）
  • everyone has something to learn —— 不定代词 everyone / something
  • I don't know what to do —— 疑问词 + 动词不定式（what to do）
Essay 4

A Wonderful Place

98 words

对应 Unit 4 The Wonders of Nature（自然奇观）  语法重点：被动语态 · 数词

写作提示

  • 介绍一处自然景点（桂林 / 黄山 / 长城），说它在哪里、长什么样。
  • 重点用被动语态：be covered with / be known as / be visited by。
  • 写一件在那里做的事：坐船、爬山、拍照。
  • 结尾表达感受，并说“想再去一次”。

Last summer, I went to Guilin with my parents. Guilin is a wonderful place. The mountains there are covered with green trees. The water is clean and clear. Guilin is known as the most beautiful city in China. Every year, it is visited by thousands of tourists. We took a boat on the Li River. From the boat, we could see many hills. Some of them look like animals. My mother told me that the mountains were formed many years ago. I took many photos there. I love Guilin, and I want to visit it again some day.

Picture Place
（可贴照片或自己画图）

亮点句型 Highlighted Sentences

  • The mountains are covered with green trees —— 被动语态 be covered with“被……覆盖”
  • Guilin is known as the most beautiful city in China —— 被动语态 be known as“以……闻名”
  • it is visited by thousands of tourists —— 被动语态 be visited by + 动作发出者
  • the mountains were formed many years ago —— 一般过去时的被动语态
Essay 5

A Stormy Day

116 words

对应 Unit 5 Nature's Temper（自然灾害）  语法重点：过去进行时 · when / while

写作提示

  • 写一次下雨或台风天的经历，全篇用过去时。
  • 用过去进行时描述“当时正在做的事”：was/were doing ... when ...
  • 用 when / while 把两件事连在一起。
  • 写天气的变化和家人的反应，结尾写天气如何好转。

Last Friday, it was a stormy day. In the afternoon, I was doing my homework at home when it started to rain. At first, it rained a little. But soon, the wind was blowing very hard, and the rain was falling heavily. While I was looking out of the window, a tree fell down on the road. I was very scared. My mother was cooking dinner in the kitchen. She told me not to be afraid. While we were eating, the light went out. Everything was dark. We had to eat by candlelight. I thought it was a special dinner. After an hour, the storm stopped. The rain was over, and the sky was clear again.

Picture Place
（可贴照片或自己画图）

亮点句型 Highlighted Sentences

  • I was doing my homework when it started to rain —— 过去进行时 + when + 一般过去时
  • The wind was blowing very hard / the rain was falling heavily —— 过去进行时描述当时的情景
  • While I was looking out of the window, a tree fell down —— while + 过去进行时
  • While we were eating, the light went out —— while 引导的时间状语从句
Essay 6

A Good Book

117 words

对应 Unit 7 A Good Read（好书阅读）  语法重点：介绍与评价书籍的常用句型

写作提示

  • 选一本读过的书（西游记 / 哈利波特 / 鲁滨逊漂流记），写出书名和主要内容。
  • 介绍你最喜欢的角色，并说明为什么喜欢。
  • 用 It is called ... / I think it is ... 等句型介绍和评价。
  • 结尾写读书的收获和接下来的打算。

During this summer vacation, I read a very interesting book. It is called Journey to the West. It tells the story of a monk and his three students. They go to the west to get some Buddhist books. The Monkey King is my favorite character. He is very clever and brave. He can change into different things, and he can fly. He always protects his master. When they meet monsters, the Monkey King fights them. Sometimes I read for an hour before I go to bed. I think the book is exciting and funny. Reading books is good for me. I can learn many new words from it. I want to read more good books this year.

（可贴照片或自己画图）

亮点句型 Highlighted Sentences

  • It is called Journey to the West —— be called 用于介绍书名
  • The Monkey King is my favorite character —— 表达最喜欢的角色
  • When they meet monsters, the Monkey King fights them —— when 引导的时间状语从句
  • I think the book is exciting and funny —— I think + 宾语从句
Essay 7

My Summer Vacation

107 words

自拟话题   语法综合：一般过去时 + 现在完成时

写作提示

  • 回忆暑假里做过的几件事：旅行、探亲、游泳、读书、写作业。
  • 已经完成的事用现在完成时：have / has done。
  • 过去某个时间做的具体事用一般过去时。
  • 结尾总结这个暑假的收获和心情。

My summer vacation is almost over. I have done many interesting things this summer. In July, I went to my grandma's home in the countryside. I helped her water the vegetables in the garden. The food there was very delicious. I have eaten a lot this summer, so I am a little heavier now. I also read two books, and I have finished them. I have learned to swim, too. At first, I was afraid of water, but now I can swim across the pool. I haven't done my homework yet, but I will finish it before school starts. I have enjoyed my summer vacation very much.

Picture Place
（可贴照片或自己画图）

亮点句型 Highlighted Sentences

  • I have done many interesting things this summer —— 现在完成时，强调已经做过
  • I went to my grandma's home in the countryside —— 一般过去时，写过去的具体事件
  • I have eaten a lot / I have finished them / I have learned to swim —— 现在完成时
  • I haven't done my homework yet —— haven't + yet，表示“还没做……”
